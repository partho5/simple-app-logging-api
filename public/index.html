How you use it

Three steps. That's the whole workflow.

No timelines to drag. No clips to trim. You only ever do this part — the tool does the rest.

Step 1

Enter a topic

Type the subject of your video in plain words — that's the only required input.

Step 2

Add hints (optional)

Want a specific angle or tone? Leave a short note on how the script should be written.

Step 3

Click "Create Video"

That's it. Faceless Studio takes it from a one-line idea to a finished video automatically.

What you get

It's not just a video file.

Every generation produces a full, upload-ready package — the video, plus everything you'd normally write yourself afterward.

Title variations

Several title options generated per video, so you can pick — or A/B test — the one that fits best.

Description

A ready-to-publish video description, written to match the script's content.

Hashtags

Relevant hashtags for the platform you're publishing to, generated automatically.

Thumbnail

A thumbnail prompt or ready-made thumbnail asset, built to match your topic.

SEO, done

Titles, description, and tags are optimized together — not written in isolation.

In short: you leave with everything needed to hit publish — not just footage to edit later.

Staying in control

Read the script before it becomes a video.

Before any footage or voice-over is generated, Faceless Studio shows you the full script. You can edit it directly, then approve it to continue.

Our recommendation: always take a minute to review it. AI-written scripts are usually good, but they don't always match exactly what you intended to say — a quick read-through catches that before it's locked into a finished video.

Behind the scenes

What actually happens after you click "Create."

One click looks simple from the outside. Here's the real sequence running underneath it.

  1. 01

    Your idea gets expanded

    The AI takes your topic and hints and builds it out into a full concept before writing anything.

  2. 02

    A complete script is written

    This is the draft you'll review and can edit before anything else happens.

  3. 03

    The system searches for footage

    It searches a large stock footage library for clips relevant to the script.

  4. 04

    Clips are blended, not repeated

    Footage is combined from many different clips, instead of leaning on one source repeatedly.

  5. 05

    Visuals are matched to the words

    Each visual is chosen to match what's actually being said at that moment in the script. Relevant motion graphics are added automatically where they fit.

  6. 06

    A voice-over is generated

    Using the voice, language, and speaking style you selected.

  7. 07

    Subtitles are created

    Generated automatically, in the subtitle language you chose — the same as the audio, or different.

  8. 08

    Key words get highlighted

    Important words and phrases can appear in a different subtitle color, drawing the eye at the right moment.

  9. 09

    Everything stays in sync

    Subtitles are timed to match the spoken audio exactly, all the way through.

  10. 10

    It's all assembled automatically

    Script, footage, voice-over, subtitles, and graphics come together into one finished video — ready to review, download, or publish.
